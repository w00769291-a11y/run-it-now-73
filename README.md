# India Live

Build a **premium, modern, responsive website for SAC COMMUNITY**, a pan-India platform for discovering and participating in **sports, arts, culture, gaming, festivals, events and experiences**.

The site should feel like a **high-end national sports, culture and entertainment platform**: cinematic, energetic, editorial, youthful, community-driven and India-wide. Take inspiration from the smooth scroll-driven animation quality of modern GSAP experiences such as https://gsap.com/scroll/ — **do not copy its branding, layout or assets**.

## BRAND & VISUAL STYLE

Core message: **“One platform. Every event. Across India.”**

Use a strong **warm white/off-white + black/deep charcoal** foundation.

Accents:

* Electric red for CTAs, active states and highlights
* Very small amounts of warm gold/yellow

Use:

* Bold modern sans-serif typography
* Oversized editorial headlines
* Large whitespace
* Strong black/white contrast
* Large photography
* Asymmetric editorial layouts
* Thin dividers and subtle shadows
* Minimal rounded corners
* Subtle gradients and layered imagery

Avoid:

* Generic SaaS/dashboard layouts
* Excessive cards, rounded corners, glassmorphism or gradients
* Template-like repetitive sections
* Predominantly red backgrounds

# HERO IMAGE & TRANSITION

Hero images must be **standalone isolated cutout subjects**, never cards, rectangles, frames or normal carousel images.

**Never stretch, squash or distort images.** Preserve natural aspect ratios and use proportional scaling/cropping.

When transitioning:

* Current image travels **downward along a smooth semicircular curved path**
* Next image enters **from the right along a curved path**
* Together they create a **clockwise orbital/circular movement**
* Images remain upright and do NOT rotate
* Use natural acceleration/deceleration and slight overlap where appropriate
* Do not simply slide images horizontally

The path should be invisible.

### TEXT TRANSITION

Animate text independently but synchronize it with the image:

1. Current text subtly fades/moves out
2. Current image moves downward
3. Next image enters from the right
4. New text begins appearing
5. Incoming text matches the incoming slide
6. Headline completes with a clean reveal

Do not fade the entire hero or move the whole container.

### CONTROLS

Add minimal:
**← →**

and an indicator such as:
**01 / 03**

Controls must trigger the same curved image + text transition and remain synchronized with the scroll experience.

No fast autoplay.
